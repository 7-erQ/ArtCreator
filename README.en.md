# ArtCreator

[简体中文](README.md) | English

A desktop tool for selecting screen regions, generating AI images, and previewing them in place, bringing game asset creation into your current screen context.

## Demo

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2f2861ad-3f34-4fac-80fc-73eac947dc80

## Features

- Select screen regions and capture across multiple displays to generate assets at a chosen position.
- Use prompts directly or polish them with AI, optionally using a screenshot for context. Supports Chinese and English prompts.
- Generate from scratch, use multiple reference images, or paint a mask for inpainting.
- Connect to OpenAI, LiblibAI, and local ComfyUI, including saved ComfyUI workflows.
- Generate batches, continue editing, regenerate, and browse image history.
- Keep floating previews on top, with moving, resizing, cloning, and mouse click-through.
- Copy, save, and drag out PNGs, with upscaling and refinement. Transparent backgrounds and background removal are experimental.
- Switch between Chinese and English through the home menu's Language option. Your choice is remembered.
